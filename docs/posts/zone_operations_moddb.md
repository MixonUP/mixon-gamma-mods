## Description (paste as the page body)

**Zone Operations** turns faction quests into real operations. A faction leader gives you squads,
you lead them into a battle across several points, and what you take stays taken: a garrison moves
in, and after the fighting the faction's own people arrive: trader, mechanic, medic, barman and a
commander with their own quests.

Built and tested on **G.A.M.M.A.**, drops into existing saves.

### Clear Sky: from the Swamps to Jupiter

Unlocks after the Swamps storyline. A chain of four operations:

1. **Swamp Cleanup**: two Clear Sky squads clear the renegade camps.
2. **Operation Factory**: the Agroprom small factory is held by the army. Four groups, three
   stages: gate, yard, building. Five minutes after you take it the army counterattacks; hold it,
   and the radio brings a truce. The factory becomes Clear Sky's main base: mechanic, the trader
   Arsenal, a barman and the boss, Viking.
3. **The Scientists' Bunker** (4 days later): Sakharov's people call for help. Viking sends a rapid
   response group. Three waves of twenty elite zombified, then six Monolith squads with a legend in
   each. Hold the bunker, and the ecologists become your friends; the trader Kompas sets up by the
   bunker.
4. **Checkpoint** (5 days later, Brain Scorcher off): the Jupiter checkpoint is held by Monolith
   veterans. Take it, survive their counterattack, and it becomes Clear Sky's northern base:
   commander Kholodny, the trader Salazar, the mechanic Kruger, a medic and a barman.

### Freedom: the road north

- **Operation Outpost**: knock Duty off the Garbage checkpoint.
- **Operation Rostok**: a two-sided assault on Rostok: both outposts, Petrenko's HQ and Voronin's
  bunker. Duty pulls back to Zaton; Freedom moves in with a trader, a medic, a mechanic and the
  commander Yastreb.
- **Defense of Yanov / Operation Return**: hold Yanov against Duty, or take it back.

Duty (Operation Yanov) and the Army (Operation Valley, Dark Valley) have operations too.

### How battles work

- You command allied squads; the enemy holds the points, and counterattacks come after a capture.
- One reinforcement per stage: when three fighters or fewer are left, help arrives with a PDA
  message.
- The PDA map and minimap show where the attack comes from and the attackers themselves.
- Service NPCs walk in a few hours after the fighting, take their posts (seated at tables, by a
  workbench) and have map icons.
- Every base has a commander or trader with repeatable tasks: patrols, convoys, ambushes, hunts,
  artefacts, rare weapons and documents.

### Requirements

- S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly 1.5.3, Modded Exes, MCM: all already in G.A.M.M.A.
- Optional: Hideout Furniture (G.A.M.M.A. has it) for the furniture at the bases.

### Install (MO2)

1. MO2 → Install a new mod from archive → `Mixon_Zone_Operations.zip`.
2. Enable it at the bottom of the load order.
3. Works with existing saves; the Clear Sky chain needs the Swamps storyline done.

## Описание (RU, второй абзац страницы)

**Zone Operations** превращает задания группировок в настоящие операции. Лидер даёт отряды, ты
ведёшь их в бой за несколько точек, и взятое остаётся за группировкой: заходит гарнизон, а после
боя приходят свои люди: торговец, механик, медик, бармен и командир со своими заданиями.

**Чистое Небо:** Чистка Болот → «Завод» на Агропроме (штурм, контратака армии, перемирие, главная
база с Викингом) → «Бункер учёных» на Янтаре (3 волны элитных зомби и Монолит) → «Блокпост» на
Юпитере (северная база: Холодный, Салазар, Крюгер). **Свобода:** «Застава» на Свалке и штурм Ростока
с двух сторон. Также операции Долга и Армии.

Нужны Anomaly 1.5.3, Modded Exes, MCM (всё есть в G.A.M.M.A.). Ставится на текущие сохранения.

## 1 — The branch

```
💀 **ZONE OPERATIONS (Mixon) — THE MERCENARY BRANCH**

The mercs of the Dead City finally get a war of their own in **G.A.M.M.A.** — and a story that keeps going.

**The story so far**
The army wants the Dead City back. Dushman's mercs have held it for years, and the spetsnaz decide it's time to clear the field. You stand with the mercs — and you hold.

But someone else is watching. A day after the battle, word reaches Dushman: an informer has promised Snake's outpost at the Army Warehouses a leak about a **very secret project** for a **very important client**. Dushman sends the man who proved himself in the fight: you.

The man who comes is not a merc. He is **Sin**.

**The chain**
1️⃣ **Clean Field** — defend Dushman's HQ against the army.
2️⃣ **A Special Man** — a story task at the Army Warehouses.
3️⃣ **The Administration** — strike back and take the army's stronghold.
🪖 **Mirror:** playing army? Colonel Kovalsky sends you against the merc base.

Play as a mercenary; Dushman offers the first contract once the mercs trust you (goodwill 1000). Every radio line is voiced in **English and Russian** — pick the language in MCM.
```

## 2 — Clean Field (how it plays)

```
🛡️ **CLEAN FIELD — hold Dushman's HQ**

Dushman offers the contract. Take it, and you have **five minutes** to get ready with a minute-by-minute countdown.

**The assault**
• Two waves of army **spetsnaz**, a legend leading every squad, come out in front of the Administration.
• A separate group swings round the **flank**.
• A **Mi-24, callsign "Falcon One"** arrives over the city. It circles, fires rockets, and lays **suppression fire** — on you, on the lower floors of the HQ, on mercs in the open.

**The mercs fight back**
• 30 seconds in, a squad takes position **inside the HQ**.
• The roof team climbs up: sniper **Viper** with an anti-materiel rifle and **Sledge** with an RPG. Their job is the helicopter — they call their hits on the radio.
• The first reinforcement checks in after a minute, then one every three.
• The mercs have mined the square round the **monument**: 17 mines that go off under the spetsnaz, not under you.

**Win or lose**
Break both waves and the base is yours — downing the gunship is up to you; if it survives, it flies off. If no merc fighter is left near the HQ, or you leave the base too long, the operation fails, and Dushman offers it again in three days.

While an operation runs, **no blowouts or psi storms** start. Pay: a random **20,000–40,000**, plus goodwill.
```

## 3 — A Special Man (the story task)

```
🕵️ **A SPECIAL MAN — the story task**

A day after Clean Field, Dushman calls you on the radio: go to the merc outpost at the **Army Warehouses**, see the commander and find out what's going on.

**The outpost**
The commander is **Snake** — bald, in an exoskeleton, with his men round the campfire. They're waiting for an informer who promised them a leak.

**The guest**
A minute after you reach the gate, he walks in: a **Sin messenger**, neutral for now. He walks up to Snake and preaches, in a voice you won't forget. No names, no price.

Then he finishes the conversation the way Sin does. The **blast** takes Snake and everyone near the fire. If you stood too close, it takes you too.

**The attack**
Sin comes in force — **three squads**, preaching over the radio. Two storm the outpost; the third holds the height above it: three snipers with SV-98s, three assault men and a grenadier with an RPG.
Fifteen seconds in, Dushman breaks into the radio, shouting for Snake, and sends his best — a squad of **masters**.

**After**
Hold the outpost, and the survivors walk home to the Dead City. Report to Dushman. He tells you a little: yes, there is a project, and yes, the client is very important. You'll learn the rest in time.

Pay: **20,000–40,000** and goodwill. The Administration assault unlocks after this.
```

## 4 — The Administration and what's next

```
⚔️ **THE ADMINISTRATION — strike back**
After the story, Dushman gives the order. The spetsnaz hold the Administration: squads dug in inside, and **five mines** at the entrance — these go off under you too. Three merc assault groups go in with you. Take the building and a **merc garrison** moves in.

🪖 **The army side — mirror operation**
For army players, Kovalsky sends three spetsnaz squads against the merc HQ. Now the helicopter flies for **you**, and Viper, Sledge and the minefield are against you. Dushman and his people pull back and return if the assault fails.

🔮 **WHAT'S NEXT FOR THE BRANCH**
"A Special Man" opens a story, it doesn't close one. Planned:
• **The secret project** — who Dushman's client is, what Sin knew, and why they paid with one of their own to keep it quiet.
• **Sin's answer** — the faction doesn't forget an outpost it burned.
• **More merc contracts** across the Zone — with the same voiced radio, air support and set-piece battles.

The merc line is in development and coming in the next update. Tell me what you want the mercs to do next.
```

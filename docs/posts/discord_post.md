☢ **MIXON MODS for G.A.M.M.A.** — 4 addons, built and tested on GAMMA, drop into existing saves
🧪 **Looking for testers!** Fresh release, I want to break it before it breaks your run.

🎖 **Companion Training** — turn your squad into veterans
• PDA-style training window: companions earn XP, levels and perk points
• 7 branches: Marksman, Toughness, Medic, Scout, Carrier, Commander, Dowser
• **Reanimator**: a lethal hit knocks you down instead of killing you. You fall on your back, sky above, blood on the screen, bleed-out bar. Your companion breaks off, crouches beside you and gets you up. Second knockdown in a row = death, 4 h cooldown
• **Medic** heals you at 40% HP, stops bleeding, gives antirad — or vodka 🍶
• **Dowser** hunts artefacts with a detector
• 200+ voiced banter lines and jokes (RU, 4 voices), Invictus-aware

🎒 **Backpack Upgrades** — 5-row upgrade tree at mechanics and workbenches: protection, stamina, sprint/jump, carry weight, **+1 belt artefact slot**
🧟 **Zombie Raid** — F10 or random hordes up to 90 zombified, paid per kill (150 RU novice → 1200 RU legend)
🏋 **Carry Weight** — one MCM slider, +10 to +150 kg

⚙ All tunable in MCM. Needs Anomaly 1.5.3 + Modded Exes + MCM — all already in GAMMA.

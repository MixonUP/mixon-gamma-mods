Zone Operations turns faction quests into real operations. A faction leader hands you squads. You lead them into a battle fought across several points. And when the shooting stops, the map is different: what you take stays taken. A garrison moves in, and a few hours later the faction's own people walk through the gate: a trader, a mechanic, a medic, a barman, and a commander with work for you.

Built and tested on G.A.M.M.A. Drops into an existing save.

Clear Sky: from the Swamps to Jupiter

A chain of four operations that carries a swamp faction all the way north. It unlocks once the Swamps storyline is done.

1. Swamp Cleanup

Lebedev gives you two Clear Sky squads and two renegade camps to clear. When it is over, Clear Sky garrisons stay in both. A warm-up. The Zone will not be this polite again.

2. Operation Factory

The small factory in Agroprom is held by the army. Four Clear Sky groups, three stages: the gate, the yard, the building. Five minutes after you take it, the army comes back with four squads. Hold, and the radio brings a truce.

The factory becomes Clear Sky's main base: a mechanic, the trader Arsenal at his table under a lamp, a barman, and the boss, Viking, on the sofa.

3. The Scientists' Bunker

Four days later the scientists of Yantar call for help, and Viking sends a rapid response group. This one is a defence. Three waves of twenty elite zombified. Then, from the direction of the Wild Territory, six Monolith squads with a legend in each.

"Clear Sky: the third wave of the dead! Last push - hold the bunker!"

Hold it, and the bunker stays with the scientists, the ecologists become Clear Sky's friends, and the trader Kompas sets up beside the bunker with his guards.

4. Checkpoint

Five days after the bunker, with the Brain Scorcher switched off, the road north is open. The Jupiter checkpoint is held by Monolith veterans. You get five Clear Sky groups. Take it, then survive the counterattack: four Monolith squads, five minutes after the capture.

It becomes Clear Sky's northern base: commander Kholodny in his exoskeleton and beret, the trader Salazar, the mechanic Kruger at the workbench, a medic and a barman.

Freedom: the road north

  • Operation Outpost. Lukash sends two Freedom squads to knock Duty off the checkpoint in Garbage. A Freedom garrison and services move in.
  • Operation Rostok. The big one. A two-sided assault on Rostok: both outposts, Petrenko's HQ and Voronin's bunker. Voronin and the Duty staff pull back to Zaton. Freedom moves in: a trader and a medic in the HQ, commander Yastreb in Voronin's bunker, a mechanic by the 100 Rads.
  • Defense of Yanov. Loki's warning: three Duty squads are two hours out. Hold the station.
  • Operation Return. If Yanov has fallen to Duty, three Freedom squads take it back, and Loki and Ashot come home.

Duty and the Army

  • Duty, Operation Yanov. Voronin sends two squads of Duty veterans to take Yanov. Loki and Ashot leave for the Army Warehouses; a Duty garrison and services move in, along with Colonel Mixon and his tasks.
  • Army, Special Operation Valley. Colonel Kovalsky in Agroprom sends two sections to clear two bandit positions in the Dark Valley.

The factions' plans collide: Freedom taking Rostok closes Duty's Operation Yanov, and Duty taking Yanov closes Freedom's Defense of Yanov. Pick a side.

How the battles work

  • You command allied squads. The enemy holds the points. After a capture, expect a counterattack.
  • One reinforcement per stage. In the big Clear Sky battles, when three fighters or fewer are still standing, two more squads arrive, announced on your PDA.
  • You see it coming. The PDA map and the minimap mark where the attack comes from, and the attackers themselves.
  • Bases come alive. Service NPCs walk in a few hours after the fighting and take their posts: seated at tables, by a workbench, with icons on the map.
  • Operations pay, then pause. Each one has a cash reward, from 20,000 for the Swamps to 90,000 for the Checkpoint, and a cooldown before the next.

After the war, the work

Every base you win has a commander or trader with ordinary tasks, taken through dialogue like any story NPC: clear a renegade lair or a mutant nest by the walls, set an ambush on the road, intercept a convoy, deal with a military patrol, Monolith or a Sin camp, remove a name from a list, bring back an artefact, a rare weapon, lost documents or rare mechanisms.

  • Clear Sky: Kholodny (Jupiter checkpoint), Viking (Agroprom factory), Kompas (Yantar bunker).
  • Kholodny's north: hunts for top-tier artefacts (Heart of the Oasis, Compass, Volat Emerald and other rare finds), sorties into the anomaly fields of Jupiter and Zaton with a scanner, and anomaly measurements once the north is open.
  • Courier runs between the bases: carry a sealed artefact case or a field archive from Kholodny to Viking at Agroprom, and supplies or a weapons crate from Viking back to Jupiter.
  • Freedom: Yastreb (Voronin's bunker, Rostok). No tasks against mercenaries or bandits.
  • Duty: Colonel Mixon (Yanov).

Requirements

  • S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly 1.5.3, Modded Exes, MCM. All of it is already in G.A.M.M.A.
  • Optional: Hideout Furniture (G.A.M.M.A. has it) for the furniture at the bases.

Install (MO2)

  1. MO2 → Install a new mod from archive → Mixon_Zone_Operations.zip.
  2. Enable it at the bottom of the load order.
  3. Works with existing saves. The Clear Sky chain needs the Swamps storyline done.

По-русски, коротко

Zone Operations превращает задания группировок в настоящие операции. Лидер даёт отряды, ты ведёшь их в бой за несколько точек, и взятое остаётся взятым: заходит гарнизон, а через несколько часов приходят свои люди: торговец, механик, медик, бармен и командир со своими заданиями.

  • Чистое Небо: Чистка Болот → «Завод» на Агропроме (ворота, двор, здание; через 5 минут контратака армии, потом перемирие и главная база с Викингом) → «Бункер учёных» на Янтаре (3 волны по 20 элитных зомби, затем 6 отрядов Монолита с легендой в каждом) → «Блокпост» на Юпитере (северная база: Холодный, Салазар, Крюгер).
  • Свобода: «Застава» на Свалке, штурм Ростока с двух сторон, оборона и возвращение Янова.
  • Долг и Армия: операция «Янов» и спецоперация «Долина».

Одно подкрепление на этап, атака видна на карте ПДА и на миникарте, на каждой базе есть обычные задания: патрули, конвои, засады, охота, артефакты, редкое оружие, документы. У Холодного ещё охота за редкими артефактами (Сердце оазиса, Компас, Волат-изумруд), вылазки со сканером, замеры аномалий и курьерские рейсы к Викингу на Агропром и обратно.

Нужны Anomaly 1.5.3, Modded Exes и MCM (всё есть в G.A.M.M.A.). Ставится на текущие сохранения. Вылет? Пришли appdata/logs/xray_*.log и пару слов о том, что делал.

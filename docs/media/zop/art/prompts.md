# Zone Operations (Mixon): key art prompts

Model: Seedream 5.5 (`seedream_5_5`) via Higgsfield. Settings for every image: aspect ratio 16:9,
resolution `2k` (2720x1536 PNG), background `opaque`, no reference images. Generated 2026-11-20.

14 generations in total: 12 in the first batch plus 2 regenerations (`duty_2`, `duty_3`).
Post-processing (ImageMagick crop only, no repainting):

- `duty_1.png`: cropped to 2496x1404 to remove a red star painted on a carriage at the left edge.
- `duty_3.png`: the regeneration came with baked-in black letterbox bars; cropped to the 16:9
  picture area, 2112x1188.

All other files are untouched model output.
